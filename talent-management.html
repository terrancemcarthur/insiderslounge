Before you reach out

GOOD QUESTIONS.

What should I send?

Your name, links to your channels or portfolio, your content focus, and a few lines about what you want to build next.

Do I need a certain follower count?

Include your audience size alongside your content and goals. Your introduction gives us the context to discuss fit; this page does not set a follower minimum.

Am I committing by getting in touch?

No. An inquiry starts a conversation. Representation requires a separate discussion and agreement on scope and terms.

Can I reach out if I already have management?

You can introduce yourself and discuss future goals. Review your current agreement before making any changes to your representation.
